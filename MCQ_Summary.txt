Q1: Protozoa have several types of vacuoles. The function of a contractile vacuole is to
   A. maintain osmotic balance by continuous water expulsion
   B. creates sites of food digestion
   C. contain specific enzymes that perform various functions
   D. None Of The Above
   ✓ ANSWER: A

Q2: Members of the Protozoa may motile by all of the following methods except
   A. flagella
   B. gliding by slime secretion
   C. cilia
   D. pseudopodia
   ✓ ANSWER: B

Q3: Entamoeba histolytica cysts have………..nuclei
   A. 1-4
   B. 5
   C. 9
   D. 6-8
   ✓ ANSWER: A

Q4: This type of protozoan usually has complex life cycles that take place in a host.
   A. Prokaryotic
   B. Free-living
   C. Parasitic
   D. Eukaryotic
   ✓ ANSWER: C

Q5: The members of Mastigophora move by
   A. Cilia
   B. Flagella
   C. Whip
   D. Pseudopodia
   ✓ ANSWER: B

Q6: In Amoeba and Paramecium, the cell organelle for osmoregulation is
   A. nucleus
   B. body surface
   C. contractile vacuole
   D. pseudopodia
   ✓ ANSWER: C

Q7: Protozoa are classified on the basis of
   A. locomotory organelle
   B. shape
   C. number of nuclei
   D. size
   ✓ ANSWER: A
